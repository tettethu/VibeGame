# Player Personality and Cognitive Diversity

Two complementary theoretical frameworks: Big Five personality predicts game preferences, Multiple Intelligences expands audience coverage.

---

## Big Five Personality Theory (O.C.E.A.N)

### Five Traits and Game Preferences

| Trait | High Score Preference | Low Score Preference |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **Openness** | Fantasy worlds, explore new mechanics, creative sandbox | Realistic world style, building/farming |
| **Conscientiousness** | Strategic planning, goal-oriented, perfect completion | Free exploration, casual play |
| **Extraversion** | Social games, competition, multiplayer | Single-player immersion, narrative experiences |
| **Agreeableness** | Cooperative games, helping mechanics | Confrontation, PvP, competition |
| **Neuroticism** | Low-stress casual games | High-risk high-stress, horror, hardcore |

### Core Insight

- Games are a pathway for personality expression, not a tool for changing personality
- Player motivations in games are consistent with their life personality
- Avoid the false assumption that "players can play different personalities in games"

## Multiple Intelligences Theory (Gardner)

### Eight Intelligence Types and Game Design

| Intelligence Type | Characteristics | Game Design Elements | Examples |
|------------------|----------------|---------------------|----------|
| Logical-mathematical | Critical thinking and logic | Statistics, logical reasoning, system optimization | EVE Online, D&D |
| Spatial | Object spatial visualization | Spatial navigation, geometric puzzles, 3D visualization | Rubik's Cube, Tetris |
| Linguistic | Auditory or written language | Narrative, dialogue, word puzzles | D&D, Text RPGs |
| Bodily-kinesthetic | Body or physical movement | Motion controls, action reaction, physical interaction | Motion control games |
| Musical | Tone, melody, rhythm | Rhythm games, music puzzles, sound cues | Zelda: Ocarina of Time |
| Interpersonal | Interacting with others | Cooperative mechanics, social systems, team quests | Multiplayer social games |
| Intrapersonal | Self-reflection | Personal growth, moral choices, self-exploration | Card games, Solo RPGs |
| Naturalistic | Cognition from natural environment | Collection systems, ecosystems, exploration discovery | Treasure hunt games |

### Intelligence Coverage Matrix

| Game Genre | Primary Intelligence | Secondary Intelligence |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Strategy games | Logical-mathematical | Spatial |
| Action games | Bodily-kinesthetic | Spatial |
| RPGs | Linguistic | Intrapersonal |
| Music games | Musical | Bodily-kinesthetic |
| Social games | Interpersonal | Linguistic |

### Design Suggestions

- Most games leverage 2-3 intelligences
- Consider covering more to increase audience
- Intelligence types are not absolute, individuals have mixed strengths

---

## Application: Target Audience Analysis

```
Determine target player group
    ↓
Analyze group personality trait tendencies (Big Five)
    ↓
Analyze group intelligence preference distribution (Multiple Intelligences)
    ↓
Select 2-3 primary intelligence types
    ↓
Design game elements matching personality + intelligence preferences
```
