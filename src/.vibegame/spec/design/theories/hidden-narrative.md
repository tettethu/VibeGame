# Hidden Narrative Design

Design game experiences with deep hidden content, creating impactful narrative twists.

---

## Core Principle

**Formula**: Importance of the secret ∝ Apparent innocence × Completeness

**Design philosophy**: The game initially appears simple and one-dimensional, hiding meaningful themes or mechanical twists beneath the surface. This twist carries tremendous power.

---

## Classic Examples

| Game | Surface Appearance | Hidden Depth |
|------|-------------------|--------------|
| Braid | Super Mario-style platformer | Time reversal reflects the nature of love and loss |
| Super Meat Boy | Platformer | Lovecraftian horror gradually intrudes |
| Don't Look Back | Simple platformer | Deep themes of Greek myth's Orpheus |

---

## Four Application Areas

### 1. Spatial Design
Mazes dense with hidden passages and doorways. Examples: Demon's Souls, Dark Souls.

### 2. Temporal Design
Repeated yet unpredictable events. Example: Deadly Premonition's demon dogs after midnight.

### 3. Mechanical Design
Hidden mechanics gradually revealed. Example: In Demon's Souls, player actions unlock hidden events.

### 4. Easter Egg Design
A tradition starting from Atari 2600's "Adventure." Some easter eggs bring massive spatial expansion (like Zelda's Second Quest).

---

## Design Process

Determine core theme/secret → Design innocent surface appearance → Build complete hidden system → Design reveal pacing and clues → Validate twist impact

---

## Ultimate Effect

Reaches maximum impact when hidden secret features gradually combine to reveal a greater secret within the game world. Example: In Deadly Premonition, the map forms the shape of a dog, echoing the demon dogs.

---

## Design Checklist

| Check Item | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Surface innocence | Is it simple and harmless enough at first glance |
| Hidden completeness | Is the secret system complete and self-consistent |
| Reveal pacing | Are clues distributed reasonably |
| Emotional impact | Does the twist deliver shock |
| Consistency | Do surface and deep layers logically connect |
